Plain text becomes links in Enriched Markdown 🔗

Part two of our work with @jullerino on @t3dotchat: link recognition.

Pass a regex and things like #1284, @mentions or `src/App.tsx` turn into native links on iOS & Android. No Markdown rewriting, copy stays untouched, and they work with pills, variants and context menus out of the box.

Ready for testing in the nightly release! 🧪🚀
